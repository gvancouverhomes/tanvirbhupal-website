A clearer way to make the decision

Look beyond whether you can buy the home

A property can appear to meet your requirements and still create the wrong daily routine, financial pressure, maintenance burden, or long-term compromise.

Before recommending a direction, I want to understand what your family is trying to improve, what you do not want to give up, how much uncertainty you can comfortably carry, and what would make the move feel worthwhile.

Sometimes the right advice is to move forward. Sometimes it is to reconsider the property, change the plan, or wait until the conditions are better.

Starting points for your move

Which areas does Tanvir Bhupal serve?

Tanvir primarily helps buyers and families moving into Fleetwood, Tynehead, and Fraser Heights. He also works with homeowners selling elsewhere in Greater Vancouver when the sale is connected to a move into these areas or is otherwise a strong fit.

Can you help us buy and sell at the same time?

Yes. A coordinated move considers the sale and purchase together, including financing, deposit funds, preparation, marketability, possession dates, carrying costs, and the risks of buying or selling first.

Do we need to be ready to buy before calling?

No. The first conversation can be used to understand the market, compare possible directions, identify unanswered questions, and decide whether moving now makes sense.
