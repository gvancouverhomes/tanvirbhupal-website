Moving to a new city is hard enough. Finding the right school on top of that makes it even more stressful. One question I hear from out-of-town buyers is this: can we get our kids into Fleetwood Park Secondary for September if we are moving to Fleetwood?

The answer depends first on **where you will actually live**.

Surrey Schools determines a student's catchment school from the residential address. The district says registration is available online year-round for people living in B.C., and students begin by registering at their catchment school.

That does not mean every address in Fleetwood feeds into Fleetwood Park Secondary, and it does not mean a place at a particular school should be treated as guaranteed before you buy.

The safest approach is simple: **check the exact property address, understand the current registration rules, and confirm anything school-specific directly with Surrey Schools before relying on it in a home purchase.**

## How Fleetwood Park Secondary Registration Works for 2026-27

For the 2026-27 school year, Surrey Schools says students begin by registering at the school assigned to their home address.

You can confirm the catchment for a specific property using the official [Surrey Schools School Locator](https://www.surreyschools.ca/find-a-school).

This distinction matters for relocating families.

If the home you are moving into falls within the Fleetwood Park Secondary catchment, you begin through the in-catchment registration process.

If the home falls outside that boundary but you would still prefer Fleetwood Park, that becomes an out-of-catchment request rather than ordinary catchment registration.

According to the district's current [School Registration information](https://www.surreyschools.ca/school-registration), secondary-school out-of-catchment applications are placed on a waitlist and prioritized under district policy. Placement depends on capacity and is not something I would build a home purchase around as a certainty.

If you are comparing the two main local secondary options, my guide to [Enver Creek Secondary vs Fleetwood Park Secondary catchment differences]({{ '/articles/enver-creek-secondary-vs-fleetwood-park-secondary-catchment-differences/' | relative_url }}) goes deeper into why the exact address matters.

## Being In Catchment Does Not Mean You Should Skip the Capacity Check

This is one of the most important details for a relocating family.

Surrey Schools says that when existing or projected enrolment is high enough that a school cannot accommodate every in-catchment registration, those registrations can be placed on an in-catchment waitlist. District staff then work with nearby schools to find an available placement.

So I would not tell a buyer:

> "Buy inside this boundary and your child is guaranteed this exact school."

The better statement is:

**The address determines your catchment school, but actual placement can still be affected by capacity.**

That is why I would confirm the catchment before making an offer and then contact the school or district if attendance at one particular school is essential to your family's move.

## Fleetwood Park Secondary Is Managing Real Growth Pressure

Fleetwood Park Secondary is located at **7940 156 Street, Surrey, BC V3S 3R3**.

I grew up in Fleetwood and finished high school here in 2003. Even then, this was an area that was growing quickly. The scale is different today, but school capacity remains a practical issue families need to understand.

Surrey Schools currently lists a **32-classroom addition** for Fleetwood Park Secondary. The project received provincial approval in June 2024 and is currently listed in the design phase. The district's current project page shows a targeted tender in fall 2026 and **target occupancy in spring 2029**.

That is a target, not something I would treat as a guaranteed construction date when planning a child's September start.

You can check the latest status through the district's [Fleetwood Park Secondary addition information](https://www.surreyschools.ca/capitalprojectoffice/current-projects).

The school itself is at the centre of an established family area, but growth and school capacity are exactly why the catchment should be treated as something to verify rather than something to assume.

## What Documents Will Surrey Schools Ask For?

Surrey Schools requires documentation as part of the registration process.

The district's current registration page directs parents and guardians to provide documentation establishing items such as the student's identity and birth date, parent or guardian information, legal status where applicable, and evidence of B.C. residence.

The exact documentation requirements can change, and individual circumstances can differ, so I would use the district's current [School Registration page](https://www.surreyschools.ca/school-registration) rather than relying on an old checklist from a previous school year.

This is also why I would not tell a buyer that one particular real estate document, such as a subject-free purchase contract, automatically guarantees school registration or placement. That is a school-district decision, not a real estate one.

If you are buying from outside Surrey, keep the two processes separate:

1. Confirm the exact school's catchment using the property address.
2. Review Surrey Schools' current documentation requirements.
3. Ask the district or school how your specific moving timeline should be handled.
4. Do not remove real estate subjects simply because you believe doing so will secure a school placement.

That last point is important. Financing, inspection and other purchase conditions should be handled based on the property and your own risk tolerance, not on an assumed school-registration shortcut.

## Do Not Assume Every Fleetwood Address Feeds Into Fleetwood Park Secondary

"Fleetwood" is a neighbourhood description. It is not a school catchment.

Two homes that both feel like Fleetwood can fall into different school boundaries.

That becomes especially important when buyers start choosing a house based on one school's reputation, programs, commute or location.

Before you get emotionally attached to a property, put the **exact street address** into the Surrey Schools locator. Do not rely on the listing description, the seller, a map screenshot, or the fact that another house a few streets away feeds into a particular school.

The same principle applies at the elementary level. ## What I Would Do If You Are Moving for September

If school placement matters to the move, I would deal with it much earlier than the last week of August.

Start with the addresses.

Once a serious home enters the conversation, verify its current catchment through Surrey Schools. If Fleetwood Park Secondary is the intended school, review the registration requirements and contact the school or district if there is anything unusual about your timing or residency situation.

Then keep a backup scenario in mind.

The point is not to scare you away from buying in the catchment. It is to avoid making a major real estate decision based on a school-placement assumption that the school district itself has not confirmed.

For families with younger children, the same timing problem can show up with childcare. ## The Practical Takeaway for Out-of-Town Families

Yes, it may be possible for a relocating family to attend Fleetwood Park Secondary, but there are two different questions:

**Is your new home inside the Fleetwood Park Secondary catchment?**

and

**Can Fleetwood Park accommodate the student when you register?**

Those are not the same thing.

For the 2026-27 school year, Surrey Schools directs families to start with the catchment associated with their residential address. Out-of-catchment secondary requests are waitlisted and subject to capacity, while capacity pressure can also affect where an in-catchment student is ultimately placed.

So if the school matters enough to influence a home purchase, verify the address and registration situation first.

That is a much safer foundation for the move than buying a house and hoping the school side sorts itself out afterward.
