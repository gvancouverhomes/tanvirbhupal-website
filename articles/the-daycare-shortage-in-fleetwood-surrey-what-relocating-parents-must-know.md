For families moving to Fleetwood, childcare can be harder to organize than the home itself. The challenge is not that Fleetwood has no licensed childcare. It is that availability changes constantly by age group, schedule and provider. A centre may have space for a preschooler but not an infant. A program that works on paper may not fit your commute or school pickup time.

## Start Before You Move

Do not wait until possession day to begin calling providers. Build a list based on the exact home, work route and child's age. Ask each provider about:

- Licensed age groups.
- Full-time or part-time availability.
- Hours and late-pickup rules.
- Current fees and which provincial programs apply.
- Waitlist process and whether there is a fee.
- Closure dates.
- Before- and after-school transportation.

Join more than one legitimate waitlist, but keep your information updated and ask how often the provider wants families to check back.

## Do Not Publish One Fleetwood Wait Time

There is no reliable single wait time for Fleetwood. Availability can change when families move, children age into another program or staffing changes. Claims such as a guaranteed 12- to 18-month wait should be treated as anecdotal unless tied to a specific provider and date.

The practical message is still important: infant and toddler care can be difficult to secure, so early planning matters.

## Childcare Costs Vary

It is also misleading to give one monthly price range as though every family pays the same amount.

Fees depend on the child's age, provider type, schedule and participation in provincial affordability programs. The <a href="https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/childcarebc-programs/child-care-fee-reduction-initiative-provider-opt-in-status" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Child Care Fee Reduction Initiative</a> can reduce fees at participating licensed providers. The <a href="https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/child-care-funding/child-care-benefit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Affordable Child Care Benefit</a> is income-tested. At participating <a href="https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/running-daycare-preschool/10-a-day-childcarebc-centres/10-a-day-centres-list" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">$10 a Day ChildCareBC centres</a>, families pay no more than $10 a day, but those spaces represent only a portion of licensed childcare spaces in B.C.

Use the current provincial affordability information and confirm the actual fee with the provider.

## Before and After School Care

School-age care creates a different challenge. A family may secure a school catchment but still have no before- or after-school space.

Do not assume a program is available because it operates on or near school grounds. Providers, contracts, transportation routes and capacity can change.

Once you know the exact catchment school, contact the school and current childcare operators directly. Ask whether care is on site, whether transportation is provided and whether the program serves all grades. ## Construction and the Daily Route

SkyTrain construction along Fraser Highway can affect traffic and access, but delays vary by day and work zone. It is too strong to claim that every short drop-off will become a twenty-minute delay.

Check the Surrey Langley SkyTrain <a href="https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/current-work/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">current-work notices and weekly traffic advisories</a> and test the route during the hours you will actually travel. A daycare close to home may still be inconvenient if it requires crossing Fraser Highway at a busy time.

## Connect Childcare to the Home Search

For families with young children, I would map four points together:

1. Home.
2. Childcare or school-age care.
3. Work or regular destination.
4. Backup caregiver or alternative program.

A cheaper home can become a poor fit if the childcare route adds major daily stress. A home in a preferred school catchment can also disappoint if the care arrangement is unavailable.

Family routines extend beyond childcare too. If recreation programs will be part of your weekly schedule, my guide to [Fleetwood Community Centre facilities for families]({{ '/articles/what-facilities-does-the-fleetwood-community-centre-actually-offer-families/' | relative_url }}) covers another piece worth mapping into the move.

## My Take

Treat childcare availability as a condition of your relocation plan, not an assumption to solve after closing.

Verify the provider, fee, hours and age-group space directly. Keep a backup plan. Do not base the purchase on an old provider list or a general claim that one school has guaranteed on-site care.

Childcare is also only one part of settling a family into a new neighbourhood.
