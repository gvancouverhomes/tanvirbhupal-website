If you are comparing townhomes and detached houses in this part of Fleetwood, you are looking at two very different lifestyles, not just two different price points. I want to give you an honest breakdown so you can figure out which one actually fits your situation.

## The Space and Lifestyle Gap Is Real

Coast Meridian Estates is a locally used name for a group of estate-style homes in eastern Fleetwood. It is not an official City neighbourhood or a legally defined subdivision, and the exact boundaries are not fixed. Properties marketed under that name are often promoted for larger lots, private yards, and more separation between homes, but those features still need to be confirmed for the individual property.

If that established detached-home feel is what you are after, my guide to [larger lots and mature homes near 168 Street and 84 Avenue]({{ '/articles/where-to-find-larger-lots-and-mature-homes-near-168-street-and-84-avenue/' | relative_url }}) looks at another part of Fleetwood where buyers often compare land, streetscape and older housing stock.

Nearby townhome communities buyers may compare include The Rockwoods on 163 Street, Fleetwood Point on Watson Drive, and Fleetwood Village near 85 Avenue. Many of the homes are three or four bedrooms. You get a functional family home, but the trade-off is shared walls, a smaller footprint, and a strata council making decisions about the common property and parts of the development.

The garage can make a surprisingly big difference too. A listing that says "double garage" does not always mean two cars can park side by side. If that matters to you, see my guide to [Fleetwood townhomes with true side-by-side garages]({{ '/articles/where-to-find-fleetwood-townhomes-with-true-side-by-side-garages/' | relative_url }}).

If your kids need outdoor space and you want separation from your neighbours, the detached option wins on lifestyle. If you want less exterior maintenance and a more compact way of living, the townhomes make a compelling case.

## The Real Cost Comparison Goes Beyond the Purchase Price

The purchase-price gap between an estate-style detached home and a nearby townhome can be substantial. Current pricing changes with the individual property and the market, so compare recent sales and active listings rather than relying on one fixed range.

That price gap is obvious. What catches buyers off guard is the ongoing cost picture.

Property taxes on a detached home depend on its assessed value and the applicable Surrey tax rates for that year. The City of Surrey publishes its current [property tax rates](https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" }. You also carry full responsibility for the roof, foundation, plumbing, landscaping and the other major components of the home. There is no strata to split those costs with.

Townhome owners generally pay property tax based on the assessed value of their own property, plus a monthly strata fee. What that fee covers depends on the strata's budget, bylaws and responsibilities, so review the current documents rather than assuming every roof, repair or common expense is included.

The age and design of the townhouse matter too. Some older Fleetwood complexes offer larger rooms, more outdoor space or different parking layouts, while newer developments may offer newer building systems and finishes. My comparison of [older Fleetwood townhouses vs. new builds]({{ '/articles/older-fleetwood-townhouses-vs-new-builds-which-offers-better-value/' | relative_url }}) goes deeper into that decision.

Neither structure is automatically cheaper over ten years. It depends on what future repair needs look like on the specific property you are buying, the financial health of the strata if you are buying a townhome, and how comfortably your budget can absorb an unexpected expense.

## How the SkyTrain Expansion Affects Each Option Differently

This is the part most comparison articles miss entirely.

Construction of the Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension is underway along the Fraser Highway corridor, including guideway and station work through Fleetwood. You can check the project's [current construction information](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/current-work/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" } before judging the impact on a specific property.

Surrey has also designated Transit-Oriented Areas around the future 152 Street Station, Fleetwood Station and Bakerview-166 Street Station. These areas are subject to provincial Transit-Oriented Area requirements and the City's applicable planning and zoning framework. Surrey provides more detail on the [Fleetwood Transit-Oriented Areas](https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved/news_feed/council-designates-transit-oriented-areas-in-fleetwood){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" }.

Some nearby townhome complexes sit closer to the Fraser Highway transit corridor than the estate-style homes. That can mean easier future transit access, along with more construction activity and planned density. The exact effect depends on the address and the applicable planning and zoning rules.

Many of the estate-style properties associated with the Coast Meridian Estates name sit farther from the core Fraser Highway corridor than the nearby townhome options. Do not assume that automatically places every property outside a Transit-Oriented Area or future planning change. Check the exact address against current City and provincial mapping.

Neither position is wrong. They are just different bets on what kind of neighbourhood you want to live in ten years from now.

## My Honest Advice

Buy what you can hold comfortably for seven to ten years. Stretching your budget for a large estate lot sounds appealing until a major repair hits and your cash flow tightens.

A well-chosen townhome in this part of Fleetwood can keep you close to local schools, parks and the growing transit infrastructure without requiring the same amount of land or exterior maintenance. If you are coming from an apartment or condo and trying to decide whether that step up is enough, my guide to [moving from a Burnaby condo to a Fleetwood townhouse]({{ '/articles/moving-from-a-burnaby-condo-to-a-fleetwood-townhouse-space-vs-commute/' | relative_url }}) looks at the trade from another angle.

A detached home around Coast Meridian Estates gives you more land, privacy and control over the property, but it also puts the repair and maintenance responsibility squarely on you. The better option is the one that still feels comfortable after the excitement of the purchase wears off.
