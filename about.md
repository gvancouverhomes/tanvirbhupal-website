      <h2>Advice that looks past the surface</h2>

      <p>A home can look right at first and still cause problems later.</p>

      <p>The layout might not fit how you actually live day to day. The location might mean more driving than you expected. The monthly cost might leave you with less breathing room than you'd like. A renovation might be hiding compromises around storage, parking, privacy, stairs, or outdoor space.</p>

      <p>My job is to help you see past that first impression.</p>

      <p>I ask practical questions. I explain the trade-offs honestly. And I help you figure out whether a home or strategy actually fits your family, your finances, and where you're headed.</p>

      <p>Not every showing needs to end in an offer. Not every homeowner should sell just because the market looks good right now. Sometimes the smarter move is to wait. Sometimes it's to adjust the search, rethink the timing, or take a different path entirely.</p>

      <p>The goal isn't speed. It's making a decision you'll still feel good about once the excitement, and the pressure, have worn off.</p>
    </div>
